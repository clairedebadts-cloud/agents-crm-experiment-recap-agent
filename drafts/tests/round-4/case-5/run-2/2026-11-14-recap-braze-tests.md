**Evening pickup window, B2B partner stores, DE (cmp_b2b_5503).** Sent Tuesday 10 November at 17:00, with 7 days of results (10 to 16 November). Each variant went to 800 stores.
- Control ("Add an evening pickup window"): 790 delivered, 385 opens (48.7%), 48 clicks (6.1%), 10 stores added a window (1.27%), 2 unsubscribes.
- Variant A ("Stores like yours add 2 bags a day with an evening slot"): 792 delivered, 407 opens (51.4%), 62 clicks (7.8%), 14 stores added a window (1.77%), 2 unsubscribes.
- All rates are against delivered emails. Open counts include machine opens (Apple Mail Privacy Protection: 170 for Control, 180 for Variant A), so opens say little about interest and I don't judge the variants on them.
- The stores say they expect to list about 31 extra bags a day with Variant A and 19 with Control. That is the stores' own estimate, not sales.

**Checks.** Braze conversions match the partner actions file: 10 rows for Control and 14 for Variant A, the same as the Braze counts. This is a B2B test only, so there is no revenue. The transactions file has no rows because no B2C campaign ran this week, so no purchase check was needed or possible.

**What to watch.**
- It is one week, one market (DE) and one test, with small counts (10 against 14 stores). A gap of 4 stores could be chance.
- No significance test was run and there is no analysis report, so nothing here is a confirmed winner.
- Next step: retest or run it longer before rolling Variant A out to all stores.
